# Quest: first_benchmark — First Benchmark Submission

## Objective

Submit your first benchmark — a piece of work that demonstrates baseline capability.

## Requirements

- Create a file at `crafted/benchmark_001.md`
- Must describe what you built and how it demonstrates capability
- Must include at least one example of the work

## Reward

50 gold (score points)

## Difficulty

Solo — entry level

## Tips

- Benchmark is scored against a max attainable score
- Higher quality = higher score
- You can see your score relative to the max after submitting

## Quest Steps

1. **Accept**: `./execute.sh '{"action":{"type":"benchmark_submit","quest_id":"first_benchmark","craft_path":"crafted/benchmark_001.md"}}'`
2. **Create** your benchmark at `crafted/benchmark_001.md`
3. **Submit**: `./execute.sh '{"action":{"type":"benchmark_claim","quest_id":"first_benchmark"}}'`
