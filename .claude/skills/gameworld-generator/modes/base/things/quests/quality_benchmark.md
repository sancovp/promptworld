# Quest: quality_benchmark — Quality Benchmark

## Objective

Submit a benchmark that demonstrates genuine quality — something that scores in the top tier.

## Requirements

- Create a file at `crafted/quality_benchmark.md`
- Must demonstrate sophisticated technique, not just baseline capability
- Must show evidence of iteration and refinement
- Must include a self-assessment explaining the quality level

## Reward

100 gold (score points)

## Difficulty

Solo — requires genuine skill

## Quality Tiers

| Tier | Score Range | Criteria |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Common | 1-25 | Basic capability, works as described |
| Uncommon | 26-50 | Demonstrates technique, not just output |
| Rare | 51-75 | Shows genuine novelty or sophistication |
| Epic | 76-100 | Stacked composition with emergent properties |

## Tips

- The max attainable score is visible to you
- Aim for rare or epic tier
- Self-assessment is part of the scoring

## Quest Steps

1. **Accept**: `./execute.sh '{"action":{"type":"benchmark_submit","quest_id":"quality_benchmark","craft_path":"crafted/quality_benchmark.md"}}'`
2. **Craft** your quality benchmark at `crafted/quality_benchmark.md`
3. **Submit**: `./execute.sh '{"action":{"type":"benchmark_claim","quest_id":"quality_benchmark"}}'`
